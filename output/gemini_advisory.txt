MODEL LIMITATIONS
-----------------

The flood-risk values represent model-derived estimates and
proximity-based infrastructure exposure.

They should not be interpreted as proof that a specific road,
power asset, or location will actually flood or be damaged.

The current prototype does not provide a physically simulated
storm-surge model, and infrastructure exposure is based on
spatial proximity to predicted high-risk areas.

The risk thresholds are prototype/model-derived thresholds and
have not been calibrated as operational disaster-warning
thresholds.

The system is intended as a decision-support prototype rather
than an official emergency-warning system.
